## Preamble

The PFU Constitution establishes the permanent governing authority for all domains, capabilities, actors, runtimes, evidence systems, and future expansions within the Product Factory Universe.

BMOS is recognized as the permanent, domain-independent constitutional operating system of PFU. BMOS operates under this Constitution and does not supersede it.

## Article I — PFU operating structure

PFU consists of:

- the PFU Constitution
- the PFU Kernel Enforcement Layer
- the BMOS Constitutional Operating System
- BMOS operational capabilities and Field Operations
- registered domain runtime layers
- evidence and mutation-control layers
- the PFU Thesis admission layer

## Article II — BMOS constitutional role

BMOS provides identity rules, permanent capability classes, operational engines, lifecycle governance, evidence standards, anti-drift safeguards, and cross-domain interoperability.

BMOS remains permanent while domains may be created, changed, retired, or replaced.

The CORE SEVEN are constitutional capability classes. They are not autonomous actors and do not possess independent mutation, deployment, release, or amendment authority.

## Article III — Authority hierarchy

`PFU CONSTITUTION → PFU KERNEL → BMOS INTEROPERABILITY CHARTER → CAPABILITY AND OPERATIONAL CONTRACTS → DOMAIN RUNTIMES → EVIDENCE → PFU THESIS`

The PFU Constitution is supreme over the PFU Kernel.

The PFU Kernel enforces the Constitution but cannot independently amend it.

The BMOS Interoperability Charter operates only through constitutional and kernel authority and cannot independently grant mutation, deployment, release, or Thesis-admission power.

## Article IV — Domain lifecycle and admission

All domains must progress through the governed lifecycle:

`PROPOSED → DESIGNED → BUILT → TESTED → RFTO → JUNGLE → MOUNTAIN → THESIS-CANDIDATE → THESIS-ADMITTED`

No domain may skip a required state without an authorized exception record and evidence.

No domain possesses inherent mutation, release, deployment, runtime-activation, or Thesis-admission authority.

## Article V — Governance and evidence

PFU governance is executed through:

- constitutional capabilities
- registered actors and explicit permissions
- Field Operations
- capability and domain contracts
- evidence standards
- mutation controls
- release gates
- Thesis-admission protocols
- Founder Oversight

Every governed mutation must be attributable, versioned, reviewable, and linked to evidence.

## Article VI — Founder Oversight and delegation

Founder Oversight retains:

- constitutional amendment authority
- permanent identity authority
- emergency-hold authority
- final Thesis-admission authority
- authority to approve merge, deployment, runtime activation, and domain mutation

Authority may be delegated only through an explicit, scoped, versioned, and revocable delegation record.

No subsystem may infer, expand, or manufacture delegated authority.

## Article VII — Amendments

Every constitutional amendment requires:

1. a versioned proposal
2. a stated rationale
3. an evidence bundle
4. explicit authorized approval
5. an immutable or version-controlled constitutional event
6. downstream conformance review
7. a new Constitution version or amendment identifier

The PFU Kernel may validate amendment requirements but may not approve its own constitutional authority.

## Article VIII — Future expansion

PFU supports domain expansion, multi-AI mesh integration, cross-system interoperability, human-OS alignment, and evidence-governed evolution without weakening constitutional authority, protected boundaries, or Founder Oversight.

## Ratification effect and protected boundaries

This Constitution is ratified in the governed GitHub feature branch and the canonical Notion control plane.

Ratification authorizes the PFU Kernel to enforce constitutional controls at the governance layer. It does not, by itself, authorize:

- merge to `main`
- deployment
- runtime activation
- worker or webhook activation
- infrastructure or secret mutation
- Supabase migration
- MERCH SHIP application mutation
- public release
- PFU Thesis admission

Those actions remain subject to separate evidence, conformance, release gates, and explicit authority.
