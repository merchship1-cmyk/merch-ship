## Operational manifestation

BMOS is operationalized through Architect, Experience Studio, Implementor, Operations Coordinator, Evidence Analyst, Librarian, and Brock HQ.

Operational engines do not inherit universal authority. Every action is bounded by registry identity, domain scope, permissions, evidence requirements, and release policy.
