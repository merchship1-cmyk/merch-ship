# BMOS: The Permanent Operating Layer

**Founder Handbook — Constitutional Systems**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Canon candidate / release BLUE

BMOS is not a business domain. BMOS is the permanent operating system that powers every PFU domain.

## Permanent layers

1. **Constitutional capabilities — CORE SEVEN**
2. **Field Operations — execution engines**
3. **Capability pipeline — governed lifecycle**

These layers form the operating backbone behind MERCH SHIP, ZENZY, Heritage, and future PFU domains.

## CORE SEVEN

- **CABMM** — capability design, measurement, and maturation
- **LUGH** — foundation, presence, and growth
- **OORCK** — infrastructure, support, and optimization
- **LoR** — truth, memory, and knowledge
- **ROCK** — coordination, execution, and connection
- **ATLAS** — navigation, discovery, and guidance
- **HI** — human interface, engagement, and launch

The CORE SEVEN are constitutional capability classes. They operate through explicitly authorized human, software, or agent actors; they do not independently acquire authority.

## Capability pipeline

`IDEA → DESIGN → BUILD → TEST → RFTO LAUNCH → THE JUNGLE → THE MOUNTAIN → THE THESIS`

No capability may silently skip a stage. Accelerated emergency handling must reconcile omitted evidence before permanent admission.

## Founder principle

BMOS is permanent. Domains can be created, changed, retired, or replaced. BMOS remains the constitutional operating layer of the Product Factory Universe.

## Governance boundary

This handbook defines identity and operating doctrine. It does not by itself authorize merge, deployment, production execution, runtime activation, or Thesis admission.
