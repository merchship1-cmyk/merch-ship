## 1. Audit verdict

The original draft has a usable product direction, but it presents proposed systems as proven capabilities and makes performance claims without evidence.

| Original claim | Verdict | Correction |
|---|---|---|
| “More viral” and “more binge-worthy” | Unsupported outcome claim | Say “designed to improve hooks, continuity, and repeat viewing.” |
| “Faster story generation” | Unverified | Say “uses reusable prompts and templates to reduce drafting steps.” |
| “Keeps your scary lane fresh forever” | Absolute claim | Say “provides repeatable starting points for ongoing content.” |
| “You never run out of scary content” | Absolute claim | Say “helps maintain a reusable idea pipeline.” |
| “Fully automated” | Unsupported capability | Remove unless generation, review, delivery, and posting are actually implemented and tested. |
| “Daily videos” | Delivery ambiguity | Specify whether members receive scripts, completed videos, or publishing support. |
| “Daily thumbnails” | Delivery ambiguity | Use “thumbnail briefs” unless finished image files are genuinely included. |
| “Real mystery” and “creepy facts” | Verification risk | Use fictional mysteries or source and review every factual claim. |
| “This changed everything for me” | Implied testimonial | Use only if it is Ryan’s truthful experience. Otherwise replace it. |
| “Your system is complete” and “10/10” | Unsupported completion claim | Use the actual state: offer defined; production and delivery require testing. |

### Evidence boundary

The Scary Video Engine, Faceless Reels Engine, Horror Story Factory, and Thumbnail Factory are defined here as **content-production systems**: templates, prompts, checklists, and editorial rules. They are not represented as live software, autonomous agents, or automatic publishing systems.

### Safety and rights boundary

- Use original fiction, licensed assets, public-domain material, or properly sourced facts.
- Do not portray invented stories as true events.
- Avoid graphic gore, threats presented as real, targeted harassment, or identifiable private people.
- Do not imitate a living creator’s voice or visual identity.
- Review every asset before publishing.
- Platform approval, reach, views, sales, and revenue are never guaranteed.

## 2. Corrected system positioning

### PFU Scary Video System

A reusable workflow for turning a horror idea into a short-form script, visual brief, caption, and loop ending.

**Included components**

- Hook patterns for 10–30 second videos
- Micro-horror story structures
- Visual-direction prompts
- Narration and on-screen-text guidance
- Cliffhanger and loop-ending patterns
- Review checklist for clarity, originality, rights, and platform safety

**Supported formats**

- Micro-horror: hook → disturbance → reveal
- Fictional mystery: clue → contradiction → unanswered question
- Folklore explainer: sourced setup → interpretation → audience question
- Visual horror: ordinary image → one anomaly → delayed reveal

### PFU Faceless Reels System

A reusable workflow for creating short, voiceover-led or text-led reels without requiring the creator to appear on camera.

**Included components**

- Relatable and motivational hook patterns
- Short scripts with one clear idea
- Text-only and voiceover formats
- Caption and CTA patterns
- Visual briefs for stock, licensed, or original imagery

**Corrected hooks**

- “If today feels heavy, start with this one step.”
- “A quiet reminder for anyone rebuilding.”
- “Save this for the day you need to begin again.”
- “You do not need the whole plan to take the next step.”

### PFU Horror Story Factory

A repeatable editorial system for generating and reviewing original horror concepts. It does not promise automatic output or unlimited novelty.

**One production cycle can create**

- Three original micro-story drafts
- Three fictional mystery prompts
- Three visual briefs
- Three alternate endings
- Three thumbnail concepts

**Core story patterns**

- “The sound returned every night, but only one person remembered it.”
- “The photograph changed after each viewing.”
- “The recording knew a name that had never been spoken.”
- “The reflection moved one second too early.”

## 4. Scary Thumbnail Factory

### Purpose

Create consistent thumbnail **briefs** that a designer or image tool can turn into finished assets. This system does not claim to generate or publish finished thumbnails automatically.

### Core formula

**One subject + one anomaly + one focal light + two-to-four-word headline + open visual question**

### Visual rules

- Canvas: use the platform’s current recommended dimensions.
- One focal subject; avoid crowded scenes.
- Keep the anomaly readable at phone size.
- Use dark charcoal, cold blue, bone white, and one red warning accent.
- Headline: two to four words, high contrast, no unsupported factual claim.
- Place critical elements away from platform overlays.
- Generate imagery without embedded text; add final text during layout.
- Keep a text-free version for reuse.

### Ten thumbnail templates

| ID | Composition | Headline options | Anomaly |
|---|---|---|---|
| TH-01 | Face or mask on right; black negative space on left | “IT MOVED FIRST” | Reflection looks elsewhere |
| TH-02 | Door centred; red light beneath it | “DO NOT OPEN” | Handle turns by itself |
| TH-03 | Phone close-up; hand entering frame | “FROM MY NUMBER” | Future timestamp |
| TH-04 | Empty hallway; tiny figure at far end | “STATION ZERO” | Exit sign points inward |
| TH-05 | Old photo held under cold light | “WHO IS SIX?” | Extra person in background |
| TH-06 | Security-monitor grid | “CAMERA 08” | Same figure in every frame |
| TH-07 | Window with subject walking away | “STILL WATCHING” | Silhouette remains behind |
| TH-08 | Clock face in extreme close-up | “3:17 AGAIN” | Hands move backward |
| TH-09 | Journal page and dark hand shadow | “LAST PAGE” | Text is still appearing |
| TH-10 | Old television in empty room | “TOMORROW’S ROOM” | Screen shows missing furniture |

### Reusable image brief

```text
Create an original cinematic horror thumbnail image.

Subject: [single subject]
Setting: [simple location]
Anomaly: [one impossible detail]
Lighting: low-key cold-blue light with one restrained red accent
Composition: strong focal point, clean negative space for a short headline
Mood: tense, mysterious, unsettling, non-graphic
Style: realistic cinematic still, high contrast, readable at phone size
Exclude: gore, logos, watermarks, embedded text, copyrighted characters,
celebrity likenesses, and imitation of a named living artist
```

### Thumbnail quality gate

- The subject is understandable at small size.
- The anomaly is visible but not fully explained.
- The headline is legible and truthful.
- The image is original or properly licensed.
- No graphic gore or misleading “true story” label.
- The thumbnail matches the actual video.
- A human reviewer approved the final export.

## 5. Horror Character Universe

### Universe name: The Hollow Signal

**Premise:** A transmission appears through mirrors, recordings, clocks, cameras, and abandoned stations. Each encounter reveals part of a network that seems to remember events before they happen.

### Canon laws

1. The Hollow Signal predicts possibilities, not guaranteed futures.
2. It can copy patterns, voices, and movements, but not private memories without prior exposure.
3. Every anomaly leaves one repeatable mark: the time 3:17, Station Zero, or a reversed phrase.
4. No character has complete knowledge.
5. Each episode must work alone while adding one optional clue to the larger universe.
6. Horror stays suspense-led and non-graphic.
7. No episode may be presented as a real event unless it is separately researched, sourced, and labelled accurately.

### Character registry

#### HU-01 — The Listener

- **Role:** Main human point of view
- **Identity:** An anonymous audio restorer who hears messages hidden beneath damaged recordings
- **Visual anchor:** Headphones, waveform monitor, cold desk light
- **Fear:** Hearing a warning too late
- **Content function:** Found audio, whispered narration, audience-choice endings
- **Reveal limit:** Never show the full face

#### HU-02 — The Stationmaster

- **Role:** Gatekeeper of Station Zero
- **Identity:** A still figure who checks names instead of tickets
- **Visual anchor:** Dark uniform, silver punch tool, face obscured by cap shadow
- **Rule:** Appears only near a route, doorway, platform, or transition
- **Content function:** Recurring antagonist and crossover signal
- **Reveal limit:** Motive remains unknown during the first 20 episodes

#### HU-03 — The Early Reflection

- **Role:** Visual anomaly
- **Identity:** A reflection that completes actions one second before its subject
- **Visual anchor:** Slightly misaligned posture and reversed red thread
- **Rule:** Cannot leave a reflective surface unless invited by imitation
- **Content function:** Silent loops and delayed visual reveals
- **Reveal limit:** Never speaks directly

#### HU-04 — Mara Vale

- **Role:** Investigator
- **Identity:** A transit archivist cataloguing routes removed from public maps
- **Visual anchor:** Blue archive gloves, paper maps, brass key
- **Fear:** Discovering that she erased part of her own record
- **Content function:** Lore explanations, document reveals, longer story arcs
- **Reveal limit:** Her connection to Station Zero unfolds gradually

#### HU-05 — The Sixth Figure

- **Role:** Recurring photographic presence
- **Identity:** An extra person who appears in images of future disappearances
- **Visual anchor:** Pale outline, camera-facing posture, no clear facial detail
- **Rule:** Becomes clearer when an image is copied
- **Content function:** “Spot the anomaly” episodes and thumbnails
- **Reveal limit:** Never confirm whether it warns, causes, or records events

#### HU-06 — The Clockkeeper

- **Role:** Time-pattern antagonist
- **Identity:** An unseen mechanism that restarts events at 3:17
- **Visual anchor:** Backward clock hands and one missing numeral
- **Rule:** A loop always costs one object, detail, or memory
- **Content function:** Countdown stories and recurring loops
- **Reveal limit:** Keep the entity unseen; show effects only

#### HU-07 — The Hollow Signal

- **Role:** Universe-level mystery
- **Identity:** The connecting transmission, not a confirmed person or creature
- **Visual anchor:** Three broken waveform bars and a reversed phrase
- **Rule:** It communicates through imperfect copies
- **Content function:** Connects otherwise standalone episodes
- **Reveal limit:** Do not define its origin in the first season

### Season-one arc

- **Episodes 1–5:** Independent anomalies introduce the 3:17 mark.
- **Episodes 6–10:** The Listener detects the same waveform in unrelated recordings.
- **Episodes 11–15:** Mara Vale discovers Station Zero on removed maps.
- **Episodes 16–20:** The Sixth Figure links photographs to the missing route.
- **Episodes 21–25:** The Stationmaster begins crossing between stories.
- **Episodes 26–30:** The anomalies combine into the Hollow Signal; the origin remains unresolved.
