# Unified Commercial Engine v1

Status: `DEFINED + CODE-CONTRACTED / A2 PREPARATION ONLY / NON-PRODUCTION`

## Purpose

This specification integrates seven named commercial substrates/engines under AUTONOMOUS-OS without overstating runtime capability.

The system recognizes:

- Jungle BMOS
- Black Vein
- Teraformance
- Quantum Systems
- Mercury Engine
- Morpheus Engine
- Neo Engine

The composite commercial construct is:

`QTCE — Quantum–Teraformance Commercial Engine`

QTCE is a governed planning/composition construct. It is not evidence of tera-scale throughput, quantum-computer execution, predictive certainty, autonomous self-evolution, or production reconfiguration.

## Canonical roles

### Jungle BMOS
Adaptive multi-agent/system-behavior modeling. At v1 it can define and prepare models, inputs, constraints, and evidence requirements. It does not have authority to self-evolve policy or operate production systems.

### Black Vein
Telemetry/evidence-flow normalization. It defines provenance, signal, trace, and evidence transformations intended to make opaque operational inputs auditable. The term does not imply verified real-time or ultra-high-speed infrastructure.

### Teraformance
Performance and capacity planning. It prepares concurrency, load, throughput, latency, and resilience test plans. The name is a system identifier, not a measured tera-scale capability claim.

### Quantum Systems
Probabilistic and quantum-inspired decision modeling. This means classical software may use uncertainty models, search, scoring, stochastic methods, or quantum-inspired optimization concepts. It does not mean quantum hardware or quantum advantage has been implemented or verified.

### Mercury Engine
The governed route-selection/adaptation planner. Mercury can recommend which registered engine should contribute to a mission and prepare a route. It cannot create authority, mutate policy, or reconfigure production at v1.

### Morpheus Engine
The governed possibility/scenario engine. It produces alternative models, what-if worlds, patterns, and candidate architectures. Its outputs are hypotheses/candidates, not verified truth or authorized execution.

### Neo Engine
The governed realization planner. It converts a selected, authorized scenario into concrete PFU/MERCHSHIP/ZENZY mission-plan objects. It cannot represent planned work as realized without execution evidence.

## QTCE composition

QTCE members:

1. Jungle BMOS — adaptive modeling
2. Black Vein — telemetry/evidence normalization
3. Teraformance — performance planning
4. Quantum Systems — probabilistic/quantum-inspired modeling

Mercury is the conductor around QTCE. Morpheus provides scenario candidates. Neo converts selected candidates into realization plans.

## Mercury routing contract

A Mercury route is a prepared sequence of registered engine contributions. Example:

```text
MERCURY
  → MORPHEUS
  → JUNGLE_BMOS
  → BLACK_VEIN
  → TERAFORMANCE
  → QUANTUM_SYSTEMS
  → NEO
```

The route result is still an A2 artifact. Attempted live execution must stop at the AUTONOMOUS-OS A3 admission boundary.
